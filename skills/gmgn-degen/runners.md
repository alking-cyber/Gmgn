# Runner watchlist — why tokens ran 100x+ from $100K

Seed list for `gmgn-degen`. Built from Solana launchpad tokens launched in the 30 days before 2026-09-27 whose all-time-high market cap reached at least $300K; 161 of them launched below $200K and crossed $100K in their first 3 hours. For each token the features are taken **at the minute it first crossed $100K market cap**, so they describe what was visible then, not afterwards. Tokens that later died are kept: what matters here is that they ran.

Exceptions to "visible then": `bundle`, `rug`, `top10` and `clones` are the values GMGN reported at collection time, not at the $100K minute. Treat them as rough.

## What separated them (at the $100K minute)

| Feature (median) | Ran to $10M+ (16) | $1M–10M (94) | Stopped < $1M (51) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Age at $100K | 6 min | 6 min | 6 min |
| 5-min change into $100K | +49% | +88% | +80% |
| 5-min volume | $50K | $45K | $73K |
| Smart/KOL wallets already in | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| Bundle rate (today's value) | 35% | 12% | 8% |
| Has a Twitter/X link | 81% | 88% | 96% |
| Share launched on stonkfun | 50% | 44% | 27% |
| Dropped by a strict filter (bundle/rug/top-10 > 30% or any clone) | 14 of 16 | 81 of 94 | 39 of 51 |

Reading it:
1. **Bundling did not stop the biggest runners — it was more common among them** (35% vs 8–12%). A strict filter removed 14 of the 16. Treat bundle as a risk to size for, not a kill rule.
2. **Clones mean a hot narrative.** Several runners were the most-held token of a theme others copied (manifest: 30 clones, PAID and MASK: 9). Trade the leader, not the copies.
3. **Smart money was usually not in yet at $100K** (median 1 for the runners vs 2 for the rest). Waiting for smart money is not an edge here.
4. **Steady climbs beat vertical spikes into $100K** (+49% vs +80–88%, and the tokens that stalled under $1M had the most volume — early buyers selling to late ones).
5. **Speed to $100K did not separate them** (6 minutes in every group).
6. **Launchpad mattered in this window**: stonkfun produced half of the $10M+ runners while launching about a third of the sample.

## Limits

- Survivors only: tokens that never reached $300K are not in this data, so this explains which survivors became huge, not how many tokens die. Most launches die.
- 16 runners is a small sample; one month of one chain. Patterns can change with the market meta.
- `bundle`, `rug`, `top10` and clone counts are today's values.
- Refresh with the `runners` mode of `gmgn-degen`; newer entries are appended to the user's own watchlist file.
